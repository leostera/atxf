# Product context

- **Product:** ATXF, an open draft format (JSON Schema + JSONL) for exchanging coding-agent session transcripts.
- **Surface:** the specification website at atxf.leostera.dev. Mode: **Read**. Visitors are implementers of agents, converters and viewers who need to understand and cite the spec.
- **Brief (from Leo):** use Kumo UI; keep it simple; RFC/RFD style.
- **Implications:** document-first pages with RFC-style front matter (designator, status, version, updated, author, conventions), numbered sections, a contents sidebar, plain tables and code. No marketing hero, no feature cards. Kumo's tokens, components and system typography; light and dark follow the system setting.
- **Assumptions (not confirmed by Leo):** an audience of implementers rather than end users; the spec text in `SPEC.md` is canonical, and the site only renders it.
